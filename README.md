# 🚀 StratSynth AI
**Strategic Literature Synthesis & Multi-Agent Consensus Pipeline**

StratSynth AI is an advanced, Python-based research tool designed to automate **Systematic Literature Reviews (SLR)** in the field of Strategic Management and Organizational Leadership. 

By operationalizing cutting-edge methodologies from the *Strategic Management Journal (SMJ)*, this tool bridges the gap between traditional qualitative synthesis and state-of-the-art Generative AI.

---

## 🧠 Scientific Foundation
StratSynth AI is not just a scraper; it's a methodology-driven engine. It implements two foundational pillars for academic robustness:

1. **Prompt Sensitivity Analysis** (Inspired by *Carlson & Burbano, 2024*): To ensure results are not artifacts of specific wording, StratSynth runs multiple prompt variations for each Research Question (RQ).
2. **Multi-Model Aggregation & Bias Mitigation** (Inspired by *Doshi et al., 2024*): To eliminate "Artificial Evaluator" bias, the tool aggregates insights from a "Council of Models" including **GPT-4o, Claude 3.5, Gemini 1.5, and DeepSeek**.

---

## ✨ Key Features
- **📂 Batch PDF Processing:** Seamlessly converts and cleans hundreds of academic PDFs into AI-ready text.
- **🤖 Multi-Agent Consensus:** Queries multiple LLMs simultaneously to reach a "Majority Voting" or "Consensus" on complex theoretical concepts.
- **📊 Automated Synthesis:** Extracts Definitions (RQ1), Theoretical Lenses (RQ2), Contexts (RQ3), and Causal Pathways (RQ4) directly into structured Excel/CSV formats.
- **🔍 Strategic Gap Analysis:** Automatically identifies under-researched areas (RQ6) based on the synthesized database.
- **🛠️ Extensible Framework:** Designed to be easily adapted for any management or social science research topic.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Language:** Python 3.10+
- **LLM Integrations:** OpenAI API, Anthropic API (Claude), Google Generative AI (Gemini), DeepSeek API.
- **Data Handling:** Pandas, OpenPyXL.
- **PDF Extraction:** PyPDF2 / PDFMiner.
